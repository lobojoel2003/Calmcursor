# Code of conduct

Calmcursor exists to help people who find computers hard to use. Please bring
the same care to the project itself.

- Be kind and patient. Many people here live with a disability, or care for
  someone who does. Questions are always welcome.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/lobojoel2003). Reports are handled in confidence.
